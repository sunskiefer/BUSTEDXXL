# Changelog

## 0.2.1
* Module jack windows: touch a module's empty art to open a window with just its jacks, big and named; patch across two
  windows; touch empty area to close. Cables stay on the screens.
* Memory: about 515 MB of pictures (0.1.1: 622 MB). Incoming cross-screen cable stubs are shorter (FROM tag at their end);
  one show condition per picture.
* The tap-to-zoom of 0.2.0 is gone (it froze the Force). Screens are exactly as in 0.1.1.
* 938 parameters: the 924 of 0.1.1 are unchanged, then 12 module-open taps, close, and the view switch.
